Thin Ice Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Developer: Ethan Hagen

The short version

  • Your game progress is saved on your phone. We don't run servers and don't receive it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device identifiers to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

1Who we are

Thin Ice Tycoon ("the game") is made by Ethan Hagen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, who collects it, and the choices you have.

2Information kept on your device

To save your village between sessions, the game stores these on your phone only:

  • your progress: cash, businesses, upgrades, fish log, levels, pearls and settings;
  • a record of purchases (the product and the store's transaction number), so a purchase is delivered once and can be restored;
  • a short log of game events, such as finishing the tutorial or opening the store, used to improve the game during testing.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA);
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Lake → Settings → Privacy choices. On iPhone, Apple asks whether the game may track you; if you decline, the advertising ID isn't shared. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Buying No Ads removes ads from the game, though Google's ads software may still start when the game opens.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4In-app purchases

Purchases (pearl packs, No Ads, the Starter Pack and the Angler's Pass) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your name, card or payment details. The game only receives which product was bought and a transaction number, which it keeps on your device to deliver the purchase. Purchases connect to the stores through Unity In-App Purchasing.

5The Unity engine

The game is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send Unity Technologies limited technical information, such as device model, operating system and hardware details, to help Unity improve its software. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't receive personal information to share. The companies named above (Google, Apple and Unity) receive information directly from the game as described, under their own policies. Depending on where you live (for example California and other US states), you may have the right to opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising: use Privacy choices in the game where offered, limit ad tracking in your phone's settings, or contact us.

7Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

8Your choices

  • Delete your game data: use Reset save in Settings, or uninstall the game.
  • Ads: change consent in Privacy choices, reset your advertising ID, or buy No Ads.
  • Questions or requests: contact us at the address below.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
